Cases can own requested tests before collection and retain more than one sample.
Later collection attaches the existing requested work to the actual sample and
analysis without duplicating ownership; cancellation preserves the ownership
record and timeline while leaving ordinary analyses and results intact.

This is roadmap step 4, stacked directly above #4646. It evolves `MicroCase` and
`MicroCaseAnalysis` in place, adds member samples and recorded split
relationships, and keeps cases separate after transfer even when their grouping
fields match. Routing and user-facing case/access changes follow in steps 5
and 6.

### Schema and historical data

- Three changesets are registered in the real 3.6 application changelog, with
  structural rollback. No test-only schema is introduced.
- Active requested-test, case/sample, and analysis ownership have database
  constraints. Duplicate historical analysis owners halt installation for review
  rather than choosing an owner.
- Existing case IDs, specimen anchors, workflow values, analyses, results and
  issued report history remain intact. New historical
  order/type/lab-unit/Program/status and role/set/placement fields remain null
  until the reviewed clinical migration in step 16. No values are guessed.
- Related cases come from shared active samples or retained split relationships;
  sharing an order alone is insufficient.
